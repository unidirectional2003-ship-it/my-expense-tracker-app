# Expense Tracker

A simple tracker for the money that runs your home and the money that runs your small business. Household spending stays in one place. Business spending, sales, and profit stay in another. Everything is saved in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back to the same numbers.

## Features

- **Home and Business spaces.** Switch between the two so grocery runs and shop costs never get mixed together.
- **Running total.** See how much you have spent in the space you are looking at, so the full picture is always at the top.
- **Add an expense.** Record an amount, a category, a date, and a short note so every cost has a reason you can find later.
- **Home categories.** Groceries, Bills, Rent, Kids, Transport, Health, Shopping, and Other match the things a household actually pays for.
- **Business categories.** Materials, Rent, Salaries, Marketing, Delivery, Tools, and Other match the costs of running a small shop or service.
- **Checks before saving.** The form asks you to fill in every field and to enter an amount greater than zero, so a half-finished entry does not sneak into your totals.
- **Monthly repeats (Home).** Mark a home expense as repeating every month so rent, bills, and other regular costs are added again on their own.
- **Search by note.** Type a few words from a note to find one purchase without scrolling the whole list.
- **Filter by category.** Narrow the list to one category when you want to see only groceries, only materials, or only one kind of cost.
- **Edit an expense.** Fix the amount, category, date, or note when something was written down wrong.
- **Delete an expense.** Remove a cost you no longer want counted.
- **Home summary by month.** Pick any month and see that month’s household spending on its own.
- **Monthly home budget.** Set a total you are willing to spend at home this month so you have a number to aim for.
- **Money left.** See what is still available, or how far you have gone over, after this month’s home expenses.
- **Plain-language status.** A short message tells you when you are doing well, getting close to the limit, or over budget.
- **Budget progress bar.** A bar shows how much of the month’s budget is already used, so you can see the pace at a glance.
- **Daily limit.** See how much you can spend per day for the rest of the month and still stay inside the budget.
- **Spending by category.** Home expenses for the chosen month are grouped and ranked, so you can see which category took the most.
- **Savings goal.** Name a goal, set a target, and move this month’s leftover home money toward it, with a bar that shows how close you are.
- **Income and sales (Business).** Log a sale with an amount, a date, a note, and an optional customer name so money coming in is recorded too.
- **Delete a sale.** Remove a sale that was entered by mistake so profit stays accurate.
- **Profit by month.** For any month, see total income, total business expenses, and the profit left after those costs.
- **Saved in this browser.** Your expenses, sales, budgets, and savings goal stay on this device after you close the page, with no account to sign into.
- **Works on a phone.** The layout fits a smaller screen, so you can add a cost while you are out.

## How to use

1. Open the app and stay on **Home** for household costs, or switch to **Business** for the shop.
2. Add an expense with an amount, a category, a date, and a note. On Home, tick **Repeats every month** for bills that come back.
3. Use **Search** and **Category** to find an entry. Use **Edit** or **Delete** when something needs to change.
4. On Home, open **Summary**, pick a month, and save a budget. Check money left, your daily limit, and spending by category.
5. On Business, open **Income / Sales** to record money coming in, then open **Profit** and pick a month to see what you kept.
